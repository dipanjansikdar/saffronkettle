OUR STORY

A Taste of India.
Beyond the Familiar.

Indian food is incredibly diverse. From the rich and aromatic flavours of the north to the vibrant cuisines of the west, the subtle dishes of the east, the fiery flavours of the south and the countless regional traditions in between, India offers far more than one style of cooking.

Rooted in Bengal.
Shaped by India.
Inspired by discovery.
Conceived in Sudipta’s Kitchen.

But they are only the beginning.

At Saffron Kettle, we want to take you further — from the familiar to the unexpected, from well-loved classics to regional flavours and dishes that deserve to be discovered.

WHAT WE SERVE

Beloved Classics

The dishes that have helped introduce Indian food to the world — curry, tikka, butter chicken and other familiar favourites — prepared with the warmth and care of a homemade kitchen.

Regional Stories

India's culinary traditions are incredibly diverse, shaped by different regions, communities, cultures, ingredients and generations. We bring those stories to the table, one dish at a time.

Bengali at Heart

Our own roots are in Bengal — a cuisine known for its delicate balance of spices, distinctive flavours and dishes deeply connected to home, family and memory.

Beyond the Familiar

There is always something new to discover. Lesser-known dishes, unexpected flavours and regional specialities that invite you to look beyond what you already know as "Indian food."

Not just the Indian food you know.

The Indian food you haven't discovered yet.
