OUR STORY

A Taste of India.
Beyond the Familiar.

Indian food is incredibly diverse. From the rich and aromatic flavours of the north to the vibrant cuisines of the west, the subtle dishes of the east, the fiery flavours of the south and the countless regional traditions in between, India offers far more than one style of cooking.
